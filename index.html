About Me

Hi there! My name is Yussif Ibrahim-Anyass, a second-year graduate student studying tropical weather at the University of Washington under the advisory of Shuyi Chen. I am broadly interested in tropical weather and climate, the weather-climate continuum, and machine learning. I am currently working on the multiscale variability of the Atlantic ITCZ.
